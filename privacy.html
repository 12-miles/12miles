Privacy

Your privacy, in plain language.

What this website collects about you, what we do with it, and how to ask us to stop.

Last updated: November 2026

The short version

  • This website sets no cookies and runs no analytics or advertising trackers.
  • We only learn who you are if you choose to write to us.
  • We never sell or share personal information.
  • You can ask us to delete what we hold about you at any time.

What we collect

When you browse: nothing that identifies you. Fonts, images and scripts are served from this website itself, so your visit is not reported to any third party. Our hosting provider may keep standard technical logs (such as IP address and time of request) for security.

When you contact us: the forms on our Contact and Get involved pages prepare a message in your own email application. Nothing is sent until you press send, and we receive only what you chose to write — typically your name, email address and message.

How we use it

To reply to you, and to coordinate the activity you asked about — volunteering, a partnership, a donation or a programme request. We keep correspondence only as long as we need it for that purpose.

Photographs

We ask for consent before photographing our programmes and again before publishing. If you appear in a photograph on this website and want it removed, tell us and we will take it down. You do not need to give a reason. More on this in our photography and consent note.

Your rights

You can ask to see the personal information we hold about you, to correct it, or to have it deleted. Email hello@eaydi.org and we will respond as quickly as we can.

Changes to this page

If how we handle information changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
