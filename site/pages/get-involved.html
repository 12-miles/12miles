Get involved

Four ways in. Pick the one that fits.

Sustainable impact is achieved through partnership and collective action — it is one of our core values, and it is also just true.

Volunteer

Give your time to an outreach, a teaching session or the logistics that make both possible. Volunteers are trained, supported and trusted with real responsibility — many go on to lead their own sessions.

Tell us how you want to help

One short form for volunteers, ambassadors and anyone who wants to be on the list for the next outreach.

We read every submission and reply personally — usually within a few working days. If you are writing on behalf of an organisation, mention it and we will route you to the right person.

What to expect

  • A reply from a real person, not an autoresponder
  • An orientation call before your first outreach
  • Training, materials and a team lead to work with
  • No cost to you — volunteering with EAYDI is never paid for

We use your details only to respond to you and to coordinate the activity you signed up for. We never sell or share them.
